# Hakone — May 25–26

Hotel: Kinnotake Tonosawa (1 night)  
**Dinner served at hotel: 5:30pm or 7:30pm — don't miss it or you lose your slot**

---

## May 25 (Mon) — Arrive + Hakone Loop

**Getting there:**
- 9:22am — Arrive Hakone-Yumoto via Romance car from Shinjuku
- Bus (T-Line from Platform 4 toward Togendai, or H-Line from Platform 3 toward Moto-Hakone/Hakone-machi) — get off at "Deyama" stop (~25 min)
- Or taxi (~5 min) from station, or arrange hotel shuttle

**The Hakone Loop:**
1. Hotel shuttle to **Gora Station**
2. Cable car Gora → **Owakudani** (active volcano — try the black eggs and black curry chips, get souvenirs here)
3. Ropeway toward **Lake Ashi**
4. **Lake Ashi cruise** — stunning, to Motohakone
5. Walk around **Motohakone** (bigger town, right on the lake — explore a bit)
6. Return to hotel to relax before dinner

**Dinner at Kinnotake Tonosawa:** 5:30pm or 7:30pm seating — choose when checking in

---

## May 26 (Tue) — Early Departure to Kyoto

- **6:00am** — 2 taxis from hotel (ordered by hotel) to Odawara Station (~16 min)
- **6:56am** — Hikari Shinkansen departs Odawara → Kyoto 9:06am
  - Sit on right side of train for Mt. Fuji views
  - Last row of car, 2+aisle+2 seat configuration
  - ⚠️ Book via SmartEX if not already done
