## Kyoto General Notes

**Go early everywhere.** Fushimi Inari at 7am. Arashiyama at 7am. Kiyomizu-dera before 9am. These places are overwhelmed by 10am.

**Walking route (May 26 evening):** Hanamikoji St → Yasaka Shrine → Shirakawa Canal → Maruyama Park → Kodaiji → Kiyomizu-dera. Do it top to bottom as the light fades.

**K36 bar** is a noted rooftop spot for sunset in the itinerary.
