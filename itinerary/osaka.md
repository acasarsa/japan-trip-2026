## May 30 (Sat) — Full Day Osaka

**Morning options:**
- Endo Sushi at Osaka Central Wholesale Market (5am–8am) — see [fish-markets.md](../food-bev/fish-markets.md)
- Or Sakimoto Bakery for a more relaxed start

**Areas to explore:**
- **Dotonbori** — canal district, street food, famous signs, night scene
  - Street food: takoyaki, octopus skewers, ramen
  - Ajinoya — incredible okonomiyaki (long line, worth it — per friends)
- **Shinsaibashi** (Chuo Ward) — shops, malls, Sushiro conveyor belt sushi
- **Shinsekai** — classic retro neighborhood, Tsutenkaku Tower
- **Kuromon Ichiba** (if not done May 29)
- **Doguyasuji Shotengai** — ceramics and souvenirs (friends picked up a matcha set for under ¥5,000 here)

**Day trip option:**
- **Nara** — 30-45 min from Osaka. Deer everywhere. Todai-ji temple (massive Buddha).
  - Genkishin ramen (friends: best ramen of the trip — get spicy chicken)
  - Nakatanidou — peanut-dusted, red bean-filled, green tea mochi (friends: incredible)
- **Himeji Castle** — accessible via bullet train from Osaka (~30 min)

## Osaka Notes

- **Dotonbori and Ebisu Bridge are best at night** — plan at least one evening there
- **Hozenji Temple** is a quiet, lantern-lit shrine tucked into the Dotonbori area — worth a 10-min detour at night
- Friends noted Osaka is often underrated vs. Tokyo/Kyoto — the food culture is genuinely different (richer, more casual, more indulgent)
