# Tokyo — May 21–25

Hotel: Daiwa Roynet Premier (check-in 2pm May 21, early checkout May 25)  
See also: [Food](../food-bev/tokyo-restaurants.md) · [Activities](../activities/tokyo.md)

---

## May 21 (Thu) — Arrival Day

- ~1:00pm — Arrive, get Suica/PASMO + pocket WiFi at airport
- 2:00pm — Check in: Daiwa Roynet Premier
- **Lunch:** Tsukemen Ramen Namiura or Chuka Soba Ginza (1 Michelin star ramen — never had a bowl like this, per Jen & Mikell)
- **Evening:** Meiji Jingu Shrine (walk from Harajuku station on southeast side of park)
- **Night:** Memory Lane (Omoide Yokocho) / Golden Gai — atmospheric alleys, yakitori stalls, tiny bars
- **Bar:** Bar Centifolia ⚠️ (needs reservation 2 weeks before — confirm ASAP)

**Nearby on arrival:**
- Shinjuku Sushi Bar Nigirite
- Hanazono Shrine
- Shinjuku Izakaya Tour (booked: 11:30am, 3 hours, hosted by Yuma & Team)
- Red Light District tour (booked: 4:30pm, 1 hour, hosted by Koki Inoue)

## May 22 (Fri) — Tsukiji + TeamLabs + Wagyu

- **Morning:** Tsukiji Outer Market — aim for before 9am
  - Tsukiji Sushicho (cheap, standing bar — Jen & Mikell ate 15 pieces each for $44, extremely high quality)
  - See [fish-markets.md](../food-bev/fish-markets.md) for full stall guide
- Zojo-ji Temple / Tokyo Tower
- Atago Jinji Shrine
- Ginza Razin (watch store)
- Lunch at Toyo's Market
- **2:00pm — TeamLabs Planets ✅** (booked: 2:00–2:30pm entry)
- Kiyosumi Teien (garden in Koto City)
- Tomioka Hachiman Shrine
- Shower at hotel
- **7:00pm — Yakiniku Ponga Ebisu ✅** (wagyu, A5 — Jen & Mikell loved it)
- **9:00pm — Bar Trench Annex ✅** (7 people, confirmed) → Bar Banten after

## May 23 (Sat) — Shopping + Baseball

- Shibuya 7/11 breakfast
- Shibuya Crossing
- **Shopping:**
  - Onitsuka Tiger sneakers
  - Vintage shops
  - Rolex/Cartier (Ginza)
- **Lunch options:**
  - Uogashi Shibuya Dogenzaka (standing sushi)
  - Hikiniku to Come (burger place)
  - Udon Shin — put name down at 11am, ~2 hour wait (carbonara udon, 1000/10 per Jen & Mikell)
- **6:00pm — Tigers Baseball Game ✅**
- **Dinner:** Ramen walk-in after game

**Shibuya area:**
- Nonbei Yokocho (Drunkyard's Alley)
- Shibuya Sky (buy tickets in advance)

## May 24 (Sun) — Asakusa + Sumo

- **Morning:** Asakusa
  - Senso-ji Temple (Tokyo's oldest; traditional atmosphere)
  - Nakamise-dori shopping street
  - Kappabashi Kitchen Tools Street (knives, ceramics, cooking gear)
  - Ameyoko Market
- **Afternoon:** Omakase option (Asakusa area)
- **Tea ceremony** option (Asakusa area)
- **Sumo Tournament Final Championship ✅** (May 24 — verify tickets purchased)
- **Dinner options:**
  - Ginza Iwa (great sushi)
  - Tsukiji Sushicho
  - Possible omakase
