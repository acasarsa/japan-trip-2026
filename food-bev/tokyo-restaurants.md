## Sushi

**High-end / Omakase**
- **Sushi Dai** — Legendary, in Toyosu Market. Take a cab at 4am, find the queue on the 2nd floor walkway. Cannot book — just queue. (Friends did this and called it the best sushi they've ever had)
- **Kayamori** — Very special, probably not on American radar. Address: 3 Chome-1-20 2階, Kitazawa, Setagaya City. Make reservations now.
- **Hakkoku** (Ginza) — 25-piece omakase. Address: 3F, 6-7-6 Ginza, Chuo-ku. Friends did this, booked in advance.
- **Udatsu Sushi** — Very fancy and unique (Nat Geo feature)
- **Sushi Tokyo Ten** — Omakase, many locations
- **Narisawa** — Kaiseki, fancy and expensive

**Amex-call sushi (hard to book, worth trying concierge):**
- Sushi Matsuura (鮨 まつうら) — 050-5456-2029
- Sushinishizaki (鮨 西崎)
- Sushidokoro Yamato (鮨処 やまと) — 03-3543-6311
- Sushi Hashimoto
- 鮨 福元 SUSHI FUKUMOTO — has availability
- Ginza Sushi KANESAKA (銀座 鮨 かねさか 本店) — has availability

**Casual / Standing**
- **Tsukiji Sushicho** — Cheap standing bar at Tsukiji, wild value
- **Uogashi Nihonichi** — Standing sushi
- **Tsujihan** — Queue at 9:30am, opens at 11. Cash only. Known for "Jewel Box of the Sea" bowl.
- **Uobei** — Extremely cheap and solid conveyor belt sushi

## Ramen & Noodles

**Michelin / Top picks**
- **Chuka Soba Ginza** — 1 Michelin star. Jen & Mikell: "epic, never had a bowl like this"
- **Konjiki Hototogisu** — Michelin star ramen (closed Sat/Sun)
- **Tsuta Ramen** — Michelin, has online reservation system
- **Naikryu Ramen** — 1 Michelin star (friends waited 2 hours)
- **Rokurinsha** — Tokyo Station Ramen Street, known for tsukemen (dip noodles)

**Ramen Street (Tokyo Station)** — A dedicated street of top ramen shops inside the station. Great for a quick high-quality bowl. Try tsukemen (noodles served dry with dipping broth).
- Rokurinsha (tsukemen)
- Soranoiro Nippon (Rainbow Ramen)
- Ramen Gyoku (Creamy Niboshi)

**Neighborhood picks**
- **Ramen Nagi** — Golden Gai location, great noodles
- **Afuri Shinjuku Lumine** — In Shinjuku station, great ramen
- **Ichiran** — Individual booths, consistent, good solo ramen experience
- **Ippudo Ginza** — Reliable chain
- **Ramen Tatsunoya Shinjuku** — Highly rated on TikTok
- **Men'ya-Musashi** — Shibuya, known for tsukemen
- **Oreryu Shio Ramen Kagurazaka** — Salt ramen specialist
- **Ramen Tomita** — Outside the city but apparently exceptional

**Ogikubo** — Described as the best ramen neighborhood in Tokyo (IG consensus):
- Harukiya (since 1949)
- Hook (miso ramen specialist)
- Koisaimen (very heavy bowl)
