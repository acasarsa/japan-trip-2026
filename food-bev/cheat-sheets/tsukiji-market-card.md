# Tsukiji Market — Quick Reference

**When to go:** Tue–Fri, before 9am  
**Closed:** Sundays + some Wednesdays  
**Cash only** at most stalls  
**Don't eat while walking** — use the ledge at each stall

---

## The Stalls

### 🐟 Unitora — Uni Specialist
**うに虎**  
Google Maps: `Unitora Tsukiji`  
Open: 7am–10pm daily  
English menu: ✅  
Signage: English + Japanese

**Order:** Miyabi Bowl ¥10,000–¥13,000 (5 regional uni side-by-side)  
**Tip:** Arrive at 7am on a weekday to walk in. Ask them to explain each uni region.

---

### 🐟 Maguroya Kurogin — Tuna Specialist
**まぐろ屋 黒銀**  
Google Maps: `Maguroya Kurogin Tsukiji`  
Open: 8am–1pm, closed Wednesdays  
English menu: ✅  
Signage: English + Japanese

**Order:** Kamatoro (collar cut — rarest piece, ¥3,800+), Three-Color Sashimi ¥5,000  
**Tip:** Watch them slice the kamatoro at the counter around 8am.

---

### 🐟 Seagen — Best Value Tuna Bowl
**せいげん**  
Google Maps: `Seagen Tsukiji`  
Open: ~8am, closes when sold out  
English menu: ✅  
Signage: English + Japanese

**Order:** Yamayuki Tuna + Uni + Ikura Bowl ¥2,400, Ultimate Tuna Bowl ¥4,000  
**Tip:** Ultimate Bowl sells out fast — queue 30 min before opening.

---

### 🐟 Sushikuni — Affordable Uni Bowl
**すし国**  
Google Maps: `Sushikuni Tsukiji`  
Open: ~8am–2pm  
English menu: ✅  
Signage: English + Japanese

**Order:** Kobore-don (overflowing uni bowl) ¥3,200–¥3,800  
**Tip:** Come after 12:30pm on weekdays — line often disappears.

---

### 🐟 Sushi Okame — Hidden Gem Standing Bar
**すし おかめ**  
Google Maps: `Sushidokoro Okame Tsukiji` (small, may not appear — navigate to Shin-Ohashi + Harumi Dori intersection and look for it)  
Open: ~8am, closes early  
English menu: ❌ Blackboard only — point and gesture  
Signage: Japanese only

**Order:** Kohada, shime saba, whatever's on the board. Nigiri ¥100–¥500/piece.  
**Tip:** Fits 4 people. Fast turnover (~15 min). Just point at the blackboard.

---

### 🐟 Aeya — Former Auction Floor Insiders
**あえや**  
Google Maps: `Aeya Tsukiji`  
Open: ~7am–2pm  
English menu: ❌  
Signage: Japanese only

**Order:** Sashimi plates ~¥500, kohada from Tokyo Bay, kaisendon on weekends  
**Tip:** Run by former tuna auctioneers from the old inner market. Ask what came in today.

---

### 🐟 Tsukiji Yaki Uo Ishikawa — Grilled Fish
**築地 焼き魚 石川**  
Google Maps: `Tsukiji Yaki Uo Ishikawa`  
Open: ~8am–2pm  
English menu: Partial  
Signage: Japanese-forward

**Order:** Nodoguro (blackthroat sea perch) grilled to order if available; seared donburi bowls  
**Tip:** Unusual in a sushi-heavy market. Good if you want something grilled.

## Phrases to Use

**Getting something:** `Kore wo kudasai` — "This one, please" (point at it)  
**What's good?** `Osusume wa nan desu ka?` — "What do you recommend?"  
**What's fresh?** `Kyou wa nani ga shinsen desu ka?` — "What's fresh today?"  
**Paying:** `O-kaikei wo onegai shimasu` — "Check, please"  
**Leaving:** `Gochisousama deshita` — "Thank you for the meal" (always say this)
