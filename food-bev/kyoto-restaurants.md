## Markets & Street Food

- **Nishiki Market** — 5-block covered market. Great street vendors (friends recommend). Come before 10am. Pressed mackerel sushi (saba oshizushi) is the Kyoto specialty — find it here.
- **Pontocho Alley** — Narrow street packed with restaurants; great for wandering
- **Hanamikoji Street** — Main Gion street; atmospheric
- **Sanjo Street** — Shopping and food

## Near Kyoto (Day Trips)

**Uji** (~30 min from Kyoto)
- Byodo-in Temple
- Known for matcha — try matcha everything here

**Nara** (~45 min from Kyoto)
- Deer everywhere — they bow back if you bow to them
- Todai-ji (massive Buddha)
- Nara Park
- Genkishin ramen (friends: best ramen of the entire trip — get spicy chicken)
- Nakatanidou (friends: peanut-dusted, red bean-filled, green tea mochi — incredible)

**Torikizoku** (from friends) — Super cheap yakitori, lots of locals. Good if you want something casual and inexpensive.
**Chaochao** (from friends) — Gyoza.
**Ryoan-ji Yudofu** (from friends) — Tofu inside Ryoan-ji temple, beautiful garden setting.
