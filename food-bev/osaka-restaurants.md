## Restaurants

**Sushi**
- **Sushi Hoshiyama** ✅ — May 29 6pm. 1F, Tillit's Hus, 1-16, Sugawaracho, Kita-Ku.
- **Endo Sushi** — At Osaka Central Wholesale Market. 5am–2pm, closed Sundays. Cash only. See fish-markets.md for full details.
- **Sushi-roku** — Omakase, on the itinerary

**Okonomiyaki**
- **Ajinoya** (from friends) — Incredible okonomiyaki in Dotonbori. Long line but worth the wait.

**Wagyu / Yakiniku**
- **Yakiniku M Yokocho** (from friends) — Great yakiniku with a Matsusaka wagyu variant not sold outside Japan. Highly recommended.

**Markets & Street Food**
- **Kuromon Ichiba Market** — ~150 shops, covered market near Namba. Best stalls: Futomasa (fugu), Shin-Uoei (fresh octopus skewers), Entoki Maguro (tuna since 1927), Maguroya Kurogin. See [fish-markets.md](fish-markets.md).
- **Dotonbori Street Food** — Famous for takoyaki, street ramen, octopus dishes. Lots of Michelin-recommended street food spots.
- **Doguyasuji Shotengai** — Great for ceramics and souvenirs; friends got a matcha set for under ¥5,000.

**Cafes**
- **Sakimoto Bakery** — Make-your-own toast, great coffee. On the itinerary.
- **Shiba Cafe** — Dog cafe, go if there's no line.
- **Chado** — Matcha, in Chuo Ward.

## Dotonbori & Namba Area

The night scene. Go after dinner. Best with lights on.
- Takoyaki everywhere — the real thing here
- Street food tour (self-guided)
- Hozenji Temple — quiet lantern-lit shrine tucked in, worth a 10-min detour at night
- Ebisu Bridge — iconic
- Namba Shrine — different design, funky, in a cool neighborhood

---

## Shinsekai

Classic retro neighborhood with Tsutenkaku Tower. More local feel than Dotonbori. Good for a wander.

## Day Trips from Osaka

**Nara** (30-45 min):
- Deer park, Todai-ji, Nara Park
- Genkishin ramen (friends: best ramen of the trip — spicy chicken)
- Nakatanidou mochi (friends: incredible)

**Himeji Castle** (~30 min by shinkansen):
- Full half-day excursion, worth it if time allows
