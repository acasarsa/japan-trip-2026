Japan Phrases 🇯🇵

Essentials Ordering What's good? At the market Paying Hotel Compliments Allergens
🙏 Essentials
すみません
Sumimasen
"Excuse me" — gets anyone's attention
🔊 Play
ありがとうございます
Arigatou gozaimasu
"Thank you very much"
🔊 Play
いただきます
Itadakimasu
Said before eating — no direct translation, roughly "I gratefully receive this"
🔊 Play
ごちそうさまでした
Gochisousama deshita
"Thank you for the meal" — always say this when leaving a restaurant
🔊 Play
わかりません
Wakarimasen
"I don't understand"
🔊 Play
英語を話せますか？
Eigo wo hanasemasu ka?
"Do you speak English?"
🔊 Play
🍣 Ordering at the counter
おまかせでお願いします
Omakase de onegai shimasu
"Chef's selection, please" — let them choose everything
🔊 Play
これをください
Kore wo kudasai
"This one, please" — point while saying it
🔊 Play
これを一つください
Kore wo hitotsu kudasai
"One of these, please" — point at the blackboard or another diner's plate
🔊 Play
もう一つください
Mou hitotsu kudasai
"One more, please"
🔊 Play
さびぬきでお願いします
Sabi nuki de onegai shimasu
"Without wasabi, please" — sabi is counter slang for wasabi
🔊 Play
一人です
Hitori desu
"Just one person"
🔊 Play
二人です
Futari desu
"Two people"
🔊 Play
五人です
Gonin desu
"Five people" — your group size
🔊 Play
🤔 What's good / seasonal?
おすすめは何ですか？
Osusume wa nan desu ka?
"What do you recommend?"
🔊 Play
今が旬の魚は何ですか？
Ima ga shun no sakana wa nan desu ka?
"What fish is in season right now?" — asking this earns respect
🔊 Play
今日は何が新鮮ですか？
Kyou wa nani ga shinsen desu ka?
"What is fresh today?"
🔊 Play
これは何ですか？
Kore wa nan desu ka?
"What is this?" — point at anything
🔊 Play
🐟 At the fish market
今日入りましたか？
Kyou hairimashita ka?
"Did this come in today?" — asking if it's fresh today
🔊 Play
いくらですか？
Ikura desu ka?
"How much is it?" — note: ikura also means salmon roe
🔊 Play
写真を撮っていいですか？
Shashin wo totte ii desu ka?
"May I take a photo?" — always ask at market stalls
🔊 Play
袋に入れてください
Fukuro ni irete kudasai
"Please put it in a bag"
🔊 Play
💴 Paying
お会計をお願いします
O-kaikei wo onegai shimasu
"Check, please" — use this, not "oaiso"
🔊 Play
現金のみですか？
Genkin nomi desu ka?
"Cash only?"
🔊 Play
クレジットカードは使えますか？
Kurejitto kaado wa tsukaemasu ka?
"Can I pay by credit card?"
🔊 Play
🏨 Hotel & getting around
チェックインをお願いします
Chekku-in wo onegai shimasu
"I'd like to check in"
🔊 Play
予約をしています
Yoyaku wo shite imasu
"I have a reservation"
🔊 Play
タクシーを呼んでいただけますか？
Takushii wo yonde itadakemasu ka?
"Could you call a taxi for me?"
🔊 Play
トイレはどこですか？
Toire wa doko desu ka?
"Where is the bathroom?"
🔊 Play
〇〇はどこですか？
〇〇 wa doko desu ka?
"Where is 〇〇?" — replace 〇〇 with any place name
🔊 Play (example: "Where is the station?")
😋 Compliments
おいしい！
Oishii!
"Delicious!" — say this at the counter, the chef will appreciate it
🔊 Play
最高です
Saikou desu
"It's the best!"
🔊 Play
いい仕事ですね
Ii shigoto desu ne
"Beautiful work" — complimenting the chef's technique directly, very appreciated
🔊 Play
また来ます
Mata kimasu
"I'll come again" — one of the best compliments you can give
🔊 Play
⚠️ Allergens
アレルギーがあります
Arerugii ga arimasu
"I have an allergy"
🔊 Play
〇〇は食べられません
〇〇 wa taberaremasen
"I cannot eat 〇〇"
🔊 Play
甲殻類アレルギーがあります
Koukakurui arerugii ga arimasu
"I have a shellfish allergy"
For serious allergies, carry a printed Japanese allergy card — verbal communication alone can miss things at a busy counter.
🔊 Play
生魚が食べられません
Namazakana ga taberaremasen
"I cannot eat raw fish"
🔊 Play
