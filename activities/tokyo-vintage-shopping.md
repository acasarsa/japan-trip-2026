## Harajuku / Cat Street

Cat Street is a ~1km walkable strip connecting Harajuku and Shibuya. The backstreets between Meiji-dori and Omotesando are where the best shops hide.

**BerBerJin** — Classic American workwear, Levi's denim, vintage jackets. Basement dedicated to denim. High curation.
- 3-26-11 Jingumae, Shibuya-ku

**& BerBerJin** — Sister store, same building. Rare/collector pieces: deadstock, rare varsity jackets.

**RAGTAG Harajuku** — Upscale secondhand: Supreme, BAPE, APC, Gucci, Dior, Prada. Two-story, 2nd floor dedicated to sneakers. Most organized designer resale experience in the city.

**Kinji Harajuku** — Large selection, affordable. Band tees, streetwear, accessories. ¥2,000–¥5,000 range.

**Chicago Harajuku** — Three locations. Best denim selection in the city — organized by color, size, cut. Also band tees, NBA memorabilia, 90s sportswear.

**Funktique** — 90s–2000s, Y2K and early internet-era styles.

**Solakzade** — Vintage eyewear only, 1800s to modern. Niche but great.

**Atmos Harajuku** — New/limited sneakers (not resale), original flagship. Wall of boxes, gallery-style. For grails and limited collabs.

**Kicks Lab** — Mix of new and rare/vintage sneakers. Gallery displays. Strong Nike, Adidas, NB.

## Shimokitazawa

Tokyo's vintage capital. Dozens of shops, very walkable, indie atmosphere, prices significantly lower than Harajuku. Can spend a full day here.

**New York Joe Exchange** — Eclectic modern secondhand, housed in a repurposed bathhouse. Allows swap/trade-ins. First Sunday of every month = 50% off (May 4 already passed; June 1 is after the trip).
- 3-26-4 Kitazawa, Setagaya-ku · Hours: 12:00–20:00

**Haight & Ashbury** — The crown jewel. High-end vintage: antique dresses, old lace, classic Western pieces 19th–early 20th century. For serious collectors.
- 2-37-2 Kitazawa, Setagaya-ku

**Flamingo** — American vintage 1940s–1980s, retro tableware, accessories. Operating since 2005. Affordable.

**Focus** — Color-coded discount tags, 30–50% off items daily. Good treasure-hunt energy. Across from New York Joe's.

**BIG TIME** — Vintage 1950s–1970s sourced from US and Europe. Strong quality control.

**CIRCULABLE SUPPLY** — Vintage Levi's specialist. Opened April 2025, 1 min from station.

**Shimokitazawa Flea Market (Shimokita Senrogai)** — Weekends. ~30 vendors, cash only. Vintage clothing, handmade crafts. Arrive early — vendors pack at lunch even when officially open.

## Daikanyama / Nakameguro

Walkable together (~10 min between them). Also walkable to Shibuya (~15 min).

**Eva Fashion Art** (Daikanyama) — Vintage designer: mostly Margiela, Chanel, Hermès. Also lingerie, shoes, out-of-print art books, European posters. Very curated. Strong for women's designer vintage.

**J'antiques** (Nakameguro) — Top-quality vintage clothing and antique interiors, 1800s–1950s. High-end, collector-level.

## Luxury / Designer Resale Chains

### Komehyo
Japan's largest luxury resale chain since 1947. Licensed authenticators for everything.

**Komehyo Shinjuku** (flagship) — 8 floors. Fl. 1: bags. Fl. 2: 2,000+ watches. Fl. 3–4: jewelry. Hundreds of Hermès items including Birkins/Kellys at any given time.
- 3-5-6 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku

**Komehyo Vintage Tokyo (Omotesando)** — Dedicated vintage apparel/accessories, rare secondhand high-fashion brands. Four-story boutique.

**Komehyo Ginza** — 銀座5-6-12, Chuo-ku

Price reality: A Hermès Kelly that's $14K in the US may be $11–12K here. Still expensive, but legitimately cheaper than Western markets.

### Brand Off
- **Brand Off Shinjuku** — 3-5-3 Shinjuku (same block as Komehyo). Two floors: bags, watches, jewelry.

### ALLU (by Valuence)
**ALLU Ginza** — Four stories. Birkins, luxury apparel.
- 5-3-14 Ginza, Chuo-ku · Hours: 11:00–20:00

**ALLU Omotesando** — Four stories (3 above + basement). Chanel, Rolex, Chrome Hearts. Basement exclusively Hermès. More boutique/gallery feel than Komehyo.

### RAGTAG (multiple locations)
Contemporary designer secondhand: Supreme, Gucci, BAPE, APC, Dior, Prada. Harajuku Cat Street location is the best for streetwear crossover.

### 2nd Street
Chain across every neighborhood. Lower curation, higher volume. Good for value hunting. Koenji branch is considered one of the better-curated locations.

## Koenji

Alt/punk/underground. Fewer tourists, cheapest prices, strong for military surplus, streetwear, and 70s–80s Americana. ~15 min from Shinjuku by Chuo Line. Not walkable to other vintage neighborhoods — best as a dedicated half-day.

- **Whistler** — American vintage 1940s–1960s · 4-30-8 Koenjiminami
- **Re'all** — Used sneakers, leather shoes, band tees, military, denim
- **Furugiya SANGO** — Dense racks, 70s/80s/2000s, classic furugiya chaos
- **Slat** — US military, denim, affordable
- **Hayatochiri** — Colorful, anime-adjacent, very Koenji

## Flea Markets (May 21–25)

**Hanazono Shrine Antique Market** (Shinjuku) — Every Sunday. May 24 fits. 25–30 stalls: coins, ceramics, vintage goods, some clothing.

**Shimokita Senrogai Flea Market** — Weekends. May 23 (Sat) or May 24 (Sun). Best for clothing and crafts.

**Yoyogi Park Flea Market** — Various organizers, most weekends. Check Instagram before going. Likely May 23 or 24. In Harajuku/Omotesando orbit.

## Practical Tips

- **No haggling in shops.** Not done in Japan, will be embarrassing. At flea markets, gentle once is fine — accept no gracefully.
- **Cash.** Major chains (Komehyo, RAGTAG, Brand Off) take cards. Small boutiques and all flea markets are cash-only or cash-preferred.
- **Weekdays** for smaller crowds in shops. Weekends for flea markets.
- **Japanese sizing runs smaller.** S–M Japan ≈ XS–S US. Factor in fitting time.
- **Authenticity:** All major chains authenticated under Japan's Used Goods Trading Act. Counterfeits are essentially nonexistent at established shops.
- **Tax-free:** Tourists may be eligible for duty-free at shops with the tax-free mark (minimum ¥5,000 per transaction). Komehyo and Brand Off participate — ask at checkout.
- **Passport:** Some luxury shops require ID for large purchases. Bring it.
- **Arrive at flea markets early** — vendors start packing at lunch even when officially listed as open until late.
