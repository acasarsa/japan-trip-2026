# Osaka — Activities & Sights

---

## Sights

| Place | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Osaka Castle** | Can't be missed. Big site — allow 1-2 hours on the grounds. Incredible scenery and history. |
| **Dotonbori** | Canal district. Best at night — lots of lights. Self-guided street food tour. |
| **Ebisu Bridge** | Best at night. 10 min taxi from Sushi Hoshiyama. |
| **Hozenji Temple** | Quiet, lantern-lit shrine in the Dotonbori area. Worth 10 min at night. |
| **Namba Shrine** | Different/funky design, cool neighborhood. |
| **Shinsekai** | Classic retro district, Tsutenkaku Tower. More local feel than Dotonbori. |
| **Shitennoji Temple** | One of Japan's oldest temples. |
| **Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine** | Major shrine, distinctive architecture. |
| **Kuromon Ichiba Market** | Covered food market, 150 shops. 9am-4pm near Namba. |

## Doguyasuji Shotengai

Great for ceramics and cooking souvenirs. Friends found a matcha set for under ¥5,000 here.

---

## Chuo Ward (Shinsaibashi area)

- Sushiro Shinsaibashi — conveyor belt sushi
- Chado — matcha
- Stores and malls

---

## Day Trips

**Nara** (30-45 min):
- Deer everywhere, Todai-ji, Nara Park
- Genkishin (best ramen of trip per friends — spicy chicken)
- Nakatanidou mochi

**Himeji Castle** (~30 min by shinkansen):
- One of Japan's most impressive castles, half-day trip

**Koshien Stadium** — Nearby, baseball game option (if schedule works)

---

## Nightlife

- Dotonbori at night — street food, lights, energy
- Ebisu Bridge area
- Hozenji Temple area (more low-key, atmospheric)
