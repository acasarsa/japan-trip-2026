## Temples & Shrines

| Place | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Fushimi Inari Taisha** | The red gate tunnel. Go at 7am and walk to the top. Elyse: go early and go all the way — most tourists stop at the first gates. |
| **Kinkaku-ji** | Golden Temple. Elyse: "our favorite of the entire trip." |
| **Kiyomizu-dera** | Bright orange temple on the hillside. Late afternoon light is beautiful. |
| **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** | Go at 7am. Leads into Okochi Sanso Garden, Tenryu-ji. |
| **Ryoan-ji** | Famous zen rock garden. Has a great tofu restaurant inside (Ryoan-ji Yudofu). |
| **Ginkaku-ji** | Silver Pavilion. Starting point for Philosopher's Path. |
| **Chion-in Temple** | On the May 26 evening walk |
| **Shoren-in Temple** | On the May 26 evening walk |
| **Kodaiji Temple** | Elyse's friend did her tea ceremony here |
| **Tenryu-ji** | Zen temple with garden, in Arashiyama |
| **Sanjusangen-do** | Temple with 1,000 buddhas (from Elyse) |
| **Kenninji Temple** | Oldest Zen temple in Kyoto (from friends' itinerary) |

## Gardens & Parks

- **Maruyama Park** — On the evening walk May 26
- **Imperial Palace / Gyoen Garden** — Walk there on arrival day (May 26 morning)
- **Murin-an Garden** — On the itinerary
- **Okochi Sanso Garden** — In Arashiyama, attached to bamboo grove area
- **Philosopher's Path** — Walk from Ginkaku-ji to Nanzen-ji. Peaceful canal path.

---

## Neighborhoods & Streets

- **Pontocho Alley** — Narrow street packed with restaurants, atmospheric
- **Hanamikoji Street** — Main Gion street, geisha district
- **Shirakawa Canal** — Willow-lined canal in Gion
- **Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka** — Stone-paved lanes in Higashiyama, traditional shops
- **Nishiki Market** — 5-block covered food market ("Kyoto's Kitchen"). Before 10am.

---

## Day Trips

**Arashiyama area (May 27 full morning):**
1. Bamboo Grove (7am)
2. Okochi Sanso Garden
3. Tenryu-ji
4. Iwatayama Monkey Park
5. Walk 20 min → Adashino Nenbutsuji

**Uji** (~30 min from Kyoto):
- Byodo-in Temple
- Best matcha in Japan — try everything

**Nara** (~45 min):
- Deer bowing at tourists
- Todai-ji (massive Buddha)
- Genkishin ramen (friends: best ramen of the trip, spicy chicken)
