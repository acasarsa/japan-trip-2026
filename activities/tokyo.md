## Shrines & Temples

- **Meiji Jingu** — Walk from Harajuku station (southeast side of park). Both Elyse and Jeanine recommend. Yoyogi Park is adjacent.
- **Senso-ji** — Tokyo's oldest temple, Asakusa. Go early.
- **Hanazono Shrine** — Near hotel
- **Atago Jinji** — Near Tokyo Tower
- **Zojo-ji Temple** — Classic view with Tokyo Tower behind it
- **Tomioka Hachiman Shrine** — Koto City

- **TeamLabs Planets** ✅ — Immersive digital art. Also TeamLabs Borderless (also worth it per Jen & Mikell).

## Shopping

- **Onitsuka Tiger** — Sneakers (on itinerary May 23)
- **Ginza Razin** — Watch store (on itinerary May 22)
- **Kappabashi Kitchen Tools Street** — Chef knives, ceramics, professional kitchen gear. Also good for souvenirs. Asakusa.
- **Noritake** — Tableware store in Ginza
- **Itoya** — Stationery, Ginza
- **Jins** — Affordable glasses (~¥40)
- **Isetan Food Hall** — Department store food floor, great for grazing and gifts
- **Ameyoko Market** — Outdoor market near Ueno, good for cheap shopping
- **Omotesando** — "Rodeo Drive of Tokyo"
- **Takeshita Street (Harajuku)** — Quirky, youth fashion
- **Vintage shops** — Shimokitazawa neighborhood is the best for vintage

## Nightlife

- **Golden Gai** — 200+ tiny bars in Shinjuku alleys. Different vibe in every bar.
- **Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane)** — Yakitori alley, smoky and atmospheric
- **Nonbei Yokocho** — Shibuya's smaller version of Golden Gai
- **Bar Trench** ✅ — Speakeasy cocktail bar. Jen & Mikell's favorite.
- **Bar Centifolia** — Reserve 2 weeks before ⚠️
- **Zero** — Club
- **Vent** — Techno club
- **The SG Club** — Shibuya

## Worth Knowing

- **O-mikuji** — Fortune telling at shrines. Paper strips, classic experience.
- **Yanaka Historic District** — "Forgotten Tokyo," walking tour feel. Snake Street, Yanaka Cemetery, SCAI The Bathhouse, Isetatsu (woodblock paper store).
- **Jimbocho** — Book town, area full of used bookstores and map shops
- **Nakano City** — Less touristy, interesting neighborhood
