# Transportation

## Getting Around: Basics

- **IC Card (Suica or PASMO):** Get at the airport on arrival. Use for subway, buses, convenience stores. Load cash onto it.
- **JR Pass:** May be worth it depending on how many bullet trains you take — research before departure or pick up at airport.
- **Cell/WiFi:** Pocket WiFi or SIM at airport on arrival. Essential for Google Maps and translation.
- **Customs:** Fill out Visit Japan Web forms online before arrival — https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/

## Hakone Loop (Day Trip, May 25)

From hotel, the classic loop:
1. Hotel shuttle drops at **Gora Station**
2. Cable car from Gora → **Owakudani** (active volcano; black eggs, black curry — try them)
3. Ropeway toward **Lake Ashi**
4. Lake cruise to **Motohakone**
5. Walk around Motohakone (bigger town, right on the lake)
6. Return to hotel for dinner (5:30pm or 7:30pm — don't miss it)
